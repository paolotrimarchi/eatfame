Privacy notice

Last updated 4 August 2026

Fame is in a limited pre-launch phase. Signing up here joins the waitlist for the first deliveries rather than placing an order today. Nothing you do here places an order, and we never ask for payment or card details.

Who is responsible

Fame is a pre-launch project run by a private individual based in Amsterdam, the Netherlands. For anything in this notice, including any request about your data, contact hello@eatfame.com.

What we collect

If you leave your email address, we store:

  • your email address;
  • the dishes you put in your basket, their prices and the total;
  • the delivery day you picked;
  • your answers to the two optional questions after signup, if you answer them;
  • the date and time, and the ad campaign parameters in the link you arrived through.

Separately, and whether or not you sign up, we measure how the page is used: pages viewed, restaurants opened, dishes added, and whether the basket was completed. Our hosting provider also records standard server logs, which include your IP address.

We do not collect your name, address, phone number or any payment information.

Why we use it, and on what legal basis

  • To email you when we launch. Basis: your consent, given when you submit the form. You can withdraw it at any time.
  • To understand whether the concept works and at what price. Basis: our legitimate interest in assessing demand before building a business. This uses aggregate behaviour, not your identity.
  • To measure our advertising. Basis: your consent, given through the banner. Nothing is shared with advertising platforms unless you accept.

Cookies and similar technology

We do not use advertising cookies unless you accept them in the banner. Specifically:

  • Necessary. Your basket and your cookie choice are stored in your browser's local storage so the page works and stops asking. This never leaves your device.
  • Measurement. Runs without cookies and without a persistent identifier until you accept. If you accept, we remember you across visits so repeat visits are not double-counted.
  • Advertising. The Meta pixel only loads if you accept. If you decline, it is never loaded.

You can change your mind at any time using the link at the bottom of the home page.

How long we keep it

Until we launch and email you, or until 31 December 2026, whichever comes first. If the project does not go ahead, we delete the list. Analytics data is kept for a maximum of 12 months.

Your rights

Under the GDPR you can ask us for a copy of your data, ask us to correct or delete it, object to or restrict how we use it, ask for it in a portable format, and withdraw your consent at any time. Email hello@eatfame.com and we will act within one month. Deletion requests are simple here: we remove your row and that is all of it.

If you are unhappy with how we have handled things, you can complain to the Dutch data protection authority, the Autoriteit Persoonsgegevens.

Changes

If this notice changes we will update the date at the top. Since the project is at an early stage, it may change as things take shape.
